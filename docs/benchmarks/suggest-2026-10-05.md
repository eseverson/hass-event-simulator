# Suggest: real-data benchmark, 2026-10-05

Harness: `scripts/benchmark_suggest.py` (issue #26), run against a read-only copy of the user's recorder on Home Assistant 2026.9.4. Each automation that fired at least twice in the window is the ground truth: its first N fires (N = 1, 3, all, at most 500) are should-fire labels anchored at their start, and its referenced entities plus 3 random distractor entities are the tracks. A suggestion **reproduces** the automation when it hits at least 90 % of the real fires (2 min before to 10 min after) and at least 90 % of its own fires are real ones (fires while the real automation was off or did not exist yet are not counted). Held-out recall is the top suggestion's recall on the fires that were not labeled. Windows end 2026-10-06 03:00 UTC.

## What was wrong, and the fixes

- **Device triggers had no entity** (`50b1bcb`, `fix(history)`): Home Assistant's `referenced_entities` leaves out a device trigger whose entity is a registry id, so the entity that fires the automation never reached the panel. 9 of the user's automations use device triggers.
- **A few labels over-fitted conditions** (`1a6753d`): every unlabeled fire counted against a suggestion before simplicity, so with 1–3 labels the miner added whatever excluded the rest of the window ("only on Tue–Thu", "only between 22:20 and 22:25", "only when sensor.docker_printr_blue_status is Exited (0) 9 days ago"). Unlabeled fires now count only within the labeled stretch; fires past it break ties and are still listed as hints. Synthetic tests: `test_a_few_labels_generalize_instead_of_fitting_conditions_to_them`, `test_one_label_suggests_the_plain_trigger`. The weekday test now labels two weeks, so the weekend sits inside the stretch.
- **Values seen once, and toggles** (`9462a3b`): suggestions keyed on a value that never comes back (`scene.lights_off` changes to a timestamp, `person.evan`'s latitude, an `input_datetime`), and toggle automations (from/to on or off) were never suggested. A free-form entity now offers only recurring values plus a trigger on any change (`to: null`); anchors that follow changes in more than one direction get one trigger listing them (`to: [off, on]`).
- **`for:` at exactly `max_lag`** (`f5763da`): "off after an hour on" fires a moment after the hour, just past `max_lag` (1 h), so it came back as the switch turning off. Lags up to `max_lag` plus the tolerance now count.

## Remaining gaps

- One trigger per suggestion: OR of triggers is never suggested (#33, 4 automations never reproduced).
- No "any change" trigger on numeric sensors (#34, the two blueprint fan controllers).
- Event, calendar and template triggers (#35).
- With the automation's own targets among the tracks, the top suggestion is often the action's effect (the light it turns on), which fires a few ms after the real trigger. The benchmark gives the miner the automation's referenced entities, targets included; in Suggest mode the user picks the tracks.
- A single label stays ambiguous: 11 of 28 reproduce, and the rest show the real fires as outside-fire hints to label.
